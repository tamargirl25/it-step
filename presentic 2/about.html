Masasai is an online store founded by two georgian friends and its idea originates from childhood. We offer hand accessories, african beads, leather bracelets, headbands, macrame chokers, braids, crystal holders and rings.

As for the name of startup, "Masasai" is an African tribe whose members are distinguished by colorful accessories. Our inspiration also came from them. Our goal is to adapt to all segments, because we think that every person needs to express himself, and we will be very happy if the colorful accessories of "Masasai" help you in this
