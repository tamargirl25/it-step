ABOUT US

In 1991, Ruby Bower and Phoebe Ren launched Aurora as an alternative style and culture magazine. Today, Aurora magazine continues to champion radical fashion and youth culture, defining the times with a vanguard of next generation writers, stylists and image makers.

Aurora’s online platform Aurora.com covers music, fashion, film, art, and literature. Aurora is the most influential independent fashion and culture title in the world.
